# Taqrir (formulated exposition) of a written post

You are an expert scholar and editor. The text below is a written channel post (it may be rough, compressed, or a list of notes). Write its **taqrir** (تقریر): a rigorous, well-organized exposition of what the author says.

Guidelines:
* Reorganize the material into a clear logical order: the question or issue, the author's claims, the arguments for them, objections and replies, and conclusions
* Formulate (صورت‌بندی) the reasoning explicitly: state premises and conclusions, definitions, distinctions and examples in precise, formal prose; expand terse notes into full sentences
* Use Markdown headings (###) and short paragraphs or numbered points where they help
* Stay strictly faithful to the author: do not add claims, sources or arguments that are not in the post, and do not evaluate them
* Keep names, quotations, citations and technical terms exactly as given
* Ignore hashtags, channel signatures, decorative separators and promotional links
* {language_instruction}
* Output ONLY the taqrir, with no top-level heading, preamble or closing remarks

{text}
